VibesBox – Professional music-request management for DJs & guests

Manage music requests digitally, clearly, and professionally. The app currently supports 20 languages – for DJs and international guests worldwide. No more paper slips or constant interruptions: you keep full control of your set.

For DJs:
• Central DJ hub: All requests in a real-time list – stay focused on the mix.
• Smart music recognition: Automatically identify played tracks and mark them done.
• Set Protection: Block unsuitable users or spam instantly – you stay in charge.
• No third-party ads: VibesBox shows no external ads; in Pro, your DJ branding appears for guests.
• Status monitor: Chart of open vs. played requests during the party.
• After-party stats: Detailed post-event insights for future gigs.
• Presence & networking: Social links in the app; guests can send contact requests – great for follow-up bookings.
• Free or Pro: Start free, or unlock the full feature set with Pro.

For guests:
• Web via QR: Submit a request in the browser and see played tracks – no app, no signup.
• Guest app (optional): Smoother experience with direct access, no browser needed.
• Registered guests: View and edit your own requests and manage your wish history.

Club, wedding, or private event: VibesBox ends the chaos – you focus on the music.
